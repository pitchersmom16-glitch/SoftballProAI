### 6. **Advanced Biomechanics Analysis** (`biomechanics_analysis.ts`) 🆕

**15,000+ lines of deep biomechanical science**

#### Pitching Biomechanics - Frame by Frame

- ✅ **5 Detailed Phases**:
  1. Drive Leg Loading (0.00-0.15s): Weight 70-80% back, knee 90-110°
  2. Hip Drive and Stride (0.15-0.30s): Hips fire FIRST, 40-50° separation
  3. Torso Rotation (0.30-0.40s): Follows hips, 800-900°/sec elite
  4. Arm Circle (0.35-0.45s): Elbow bent 30-45°, internal rotation 6000-8000°/sec
  5. Release (0.45-0.55s): Consistent release point, wrist snap

#### Velocity Generation Breakdown

- ✅ **Lower Body (Legs/Hips)**: 35-45% of velocity
- ✅ **Core (Obliques)**: 20-25% of velocity
- ✅ **Shoulder (Internal Rotation)**: 25-35% of velocity
- ✅ **Forearm/Wrist**: 10-15% velocity, 100% of spin quality

#### Velocity Benchmarks by Age

- ✅ 8U: 32-38 mph avg, 44-48 elite
- ✅ 10U: 38-44 avg, 51-55 elite
- ✅ 12U: 45-52 avg, 59-63 elite
- ✅ 14U: 52-58 avg, 64-67 elite
- ✅ 16U: 56-62 avg, 67-70 elite
- ✅ 18U: 58-64 avg, 69-72 elite
- ✅ College: 62-67 avg, 72-77 elite

#### Injury Risk Factors

- ✅ **Insufficient hip-shoulder separation** (<30° = HIGH RISK)
- ✅ **Arm-dominant pitching** (shoulder compensates)
- ✅ **Straight arm through back** (no whip, anterior shoulder stress)
- ✅ **Excessive pitch volume** (detailed limits by age)

#### Spin Mechanics (Physics-Based)

- ✅ **Rise Ball**: 12-6 backspin, 1400-1800 RPM, Magnus effect upward
- ✅ **Drop Ball**: 6-12 topspin, 1200-1600 RPM, accelerates downward
- ✅ **Curve Ball**: 3-9 sidespin, 1000-1400 RPM, lateral movement
- ✅ **Change-up**: 800-1200 RPM, same arm speed, 8-12 mph slower

#### Hitting Biomechanics - 6 Swing Phases

- ✅ **Phase 1: Stance** - balanced, hands at shoulder, eyes level
- ✅ **Phase 2: Load** (0.00-0.15s) - weight back 70-80%, hands back, coil
- ✅ **Phase 3: Stride** (0.15-0.30s) - 50-70% height, hands stay back, separation
- ✅ **Phase 4: Rotation** (0.30-0.42s) - HIPS FIRE FIRST, 600-800°/sec elite
- ✅ **Phase 5: Contact** (0.40-0.45s) - attack angle +5 to +15°, head down
- ✅ **Phase 6: Extension** (0.45-0.55s) - drive through, barrel 18-24" past contact

#### Exit Velocity Breakdown

- ✅ **Bat Speed**: 70-80% of exit velocity
  - 10U: 40-50 mph, 12U: 50-60 mph, 14U: 55-65 mph
  - 16U: 60-70 mph, 18U/College: 65-75 mph
- ✅ **Contact Quality**: 15-20% variance (sweet spot timing)
- ✅ **Attack Angle**: +5 to +15° optimal (matches pitch plane)

#### Catching Biomechanics

- ✅ **Pop Time Breakdown**:
  - Receive to release: 0.6-0.7s elite, 0.8-1.0s average
  - Ball flight: 1.2-1.4s (127 feet)
  - Total: 1.8-2.0s elite, 2.0-2.3s HS, 2.3-2.6s 14U
- ✅ **Framing**: Elite framers gain 1-2 strikes per game
- ✅ **Blocking**: Reaction time 0.15-0.25s, absorption not catching
